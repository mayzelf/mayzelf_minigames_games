🐌 The Immortal Snail

Live your life. Don't get touched.

You live in a town full of snails. They're slow and harmless...

...except one is immortal. It never stops, it's always crawling straight toward you, and a single touch kills.

You don't know which snail it is. Watch their eyes — every other snail wanders, but the immortal's gaze is always locked on you.

Run your routine: go inside to work, eat and sleep — but the snail keeps creeping toward the door while you're in there. Step back out and it might be right in your face.
