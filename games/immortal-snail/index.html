🐌 The Immortal Snail

Live your life. Don't get touched.

You live in a town full of snails. They're slow and harmless...

...except one is immortal. It never stops. It is always slowly crawling straight toward you — and if it ever touches you, you die.

You don't know which snail it is. Watch their eyes — every other snail wanders, but the immortal's gaze is always locked on you.

Run your daily routine — Work, Eat, Sleep — before the day ends. Survive as many days as you can. It only gets closer.

WASD / Arrow Keys — move  ·  Drag on touch  ·  R — restart
Normal snails just make you stumble. Only the immortal kills.
